VYLIA: privacy score & manager, Android app published by Yogi Studio. Last updated: 7 November 2026.

1. The app

VYLIA is a free app that assesses what your Android apps can know about you and helps you reduce your exposure. It has no account and no in-app purchases.

2. Minimum age

The app is meant for people aged 13 and over.

3. What the score measures

The score describes what an app can do (its permissions, the trackers in its code), not what it actually does. It is a decision aid, not a security audit or legal advice. Finding no stalkerware does not guarantee there is none: Echap's database is kept by volunteers and cannot be complete. If you think you are being monitored, contact a victim support organisation.

4. Filtering and changes

The shield and web versions may stop some features of an app or site from working (a tracked link, a sign-in, a notification). You can pause or stop the shield at any time. VYLIA never revokes a permission or uninstalls an app itself: it opens Android's settings, where you decide.
