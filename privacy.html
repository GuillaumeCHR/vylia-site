Privacy policy

Applies to the Android app VYLIA: privacy score & manager (com.vylia.privacy), published by Yogi Studio. Last updated: 9 November 2026.

In short: VYLIA has no account, no trackers and no analytics. Yogi Studio collects no data about you: your apps are analysed on your phone and the results stay there. The Internet is only used to download public databases: when you ask for it, or before the automatic scan if you turned it on.

1. Controller

VYLIA is published by Yogi Studio, an independent developer based in France. Contact: yogistudiofr@gmail.com.

2. Data collected by Yogi Studio

None. Yogi Studio runs no server for the app and receives no data from it: no account, no identifier, no list of your apps, no location, no usage statistics, no targeting ID, no crash reports. The app contains no targeting, analytics or attribution library, and it scores itself with its own grid.

3. Data stored on your phone

To work, the app stores only on your phone:

  • the analysis of your apps (permissions, trackers found, scores, badges) and a cache of the code analysis, per app version;
  • tracking over time: your score history and the installs, removals and permission changes seen from one scan to the next;
  • your points, trophies and badges;
  • the shield counters (number of blocked requests), with no list of the sites or domains visited;
  • your settings (language, alert threshold, automations, filtering…) and, if you import them, custom scoring rules.

This data is excluded from Android cloud backups and device transfers. You can delete everything at any time from Settings (Your data), or by uninstalling the app.

4. Analysis of your apps

On the phone, VYLIA reads the list of installed apps, their permissions and the class names in their code, to look for the tracker signatures of the public Exodus Privacy database, the known stalkerware of the public database of the non-profit Echap and the spyware in MVT's public indicators. None of this analysis leaves the phone.

5. Internet connections

The app only connects to the Internet to download public files as they are: when you ask for an update, or, if you turned on the automatic scan, before that scan, at most once a week (only on Wi-Fi if you chose that option). The files are:

  • the Exodus Privacy tracker database (reports.exodus-privacy.eu.org);
  • Echap's stalkerware database, MVT's spyware indicators, AdGuard's lists of trackers, companies and disguised trackers, and VYLIA's own files (scoring rules, alternatives, web versions, private DNS providers, help resources), hosted on GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com);
  • the shield's blocklists: EasyList and EasyPrivacy (easylist.to) and AdGuard's lists (filters.adtidy.org, adguardteam.github.io).

These requests send nothing about your apps. Like any connection, they reveal your phone's IP address to the site hosting the file. The "See on Google Play", "See on F-Droid" or "Open the site" buttons open the store or the browser, which handle it under their own policies.

6. The shield (DNS filtering)

If you turn it on, the shield creates a local VPN, on the phone: it receives the apps' DNS requests, answers "not found" to the tracker domains on its list, and passes the others straight to your network's DNS server, as without it. No request goes through a Yogi Studio server and no log of domains is kept: only counters are stored. The rest of the traffic does not go through this VPN.

7. Permissions

  • List of installed apps: required to analyse them. It costs VYLIA points, like any app.
  • Usage access (optional, special access you turn on): to know how long since you last opened an app.
  • VPN (optional): for the shield, with your consent in Android's dialog.
  • Notifications and run at startup (optional): for alerts and background scans, off by default.
  • Internet access and network state: for the updates you request and for the shield.

The app never asks for your location, contacts, microphone, camera or files.

8. What you choose to send

  • Sharing: images and animations are made on your phone and sent only to the app you pick. They show overall figures only, never the name of an app.
  • Export: the file with your data or your rules is created only when you ask, saved where you choose.

9. Distribution

VYLIA is published on GitHub. If you install it from an app store such as Google Play, the store may process installation data under its own privacy policy; Yogi Studio would only see aggregate statistics.

10. Children

VYLIA is meant for teenagers and adults (13 and over) and does not target children.

11. Your rights

As Yogi Studio holds no data about you, your data stays under your control on your phone: you can view, export and delete it at any time. Any question: yogistudiofr@gmail.com. You can also complain to the French data protection authority, the CNIL (cnil.fr).

12. Changes

If the app ever handles data differently, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
